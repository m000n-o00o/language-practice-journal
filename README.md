# The Language Practice Journal

A 120-day self-study journal for English, built to close the gap between understanding and speaking: three 4-week stages of daily output, followed by the start of the next cycle.

It suits learners from about A2 to C1. The method is the same at every level; only the sources change, so pick ones you understand at about 90 percent. Twelve weeks will not move you a whole CEFR level; progress is measured by comparing your day 1 recording with later ones.

**Live site:** https://m000n-o00o.github.io/language-practice-journal/

## What's inside

- **Start:** today's day number, current level, the week's focus, and any checkpoint due today.
- **Guide:** eight steps for learning on your own, built on about 70% output and 30% input.
- **Practice ideas:** 186 activities across six areas (Listening, Reading, Speaking, Writing, Vocabulary, Grammar & Accuracy), with search, a level filter and a random pick.
- **Tracker:** 120 days arranged by week. Each day logs the core block (sentences, voice note, error log), the extended block, and practice with people, plus chunks, a best sentence and a tagged error. Progress is summarised by skill.
- **Error log:** every logged error with its tag. The three most frequent tags choose next week's grammar units.
- **Resources:** the plan's fixed sources with links and QR codes.
- **Printable version:** `Language-Practice-Journal.pdf`, 35 A4 pages.

## Your data

Everything you log is saved in your own browser (localStorage) and is never sent anywhere. Other visitors cannot see it; only someone using the same browser on the same device could. The page itself loads fonts from Google Fonts, and GitHub Pages keeps standard visit logs, like any website.

Because the data lives in the browser, it can be lost: clearing browsing data removes it, private browsing does not keep it, and Safari may delete data for sites you have not opened in about a week. **Use Export backup in the tracker once a week.** To move to another device, export on one and use **Import backup** on the other.
